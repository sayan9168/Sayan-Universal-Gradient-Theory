# References & Intellectual Context

SUG is an original proposal, but it is deliberately anchored in the language
of established physics so it can be compared with, and tested against, known
results.  This list gives a researcher the classical background the theory
speaks to.  **(SUG is not validated by, nor does it claim endorsement from,
any of these works — they are context, not confirmation.)**

## Thermodynamics & thermal transport
- Clausius, R. (1865). On the motive power of heat. *Annalen der Physik* — origin of entropy and the framing of heat as a state function.
- Standard references on the laws of thermodynamics and heat transport across gradients (temperature as driver of flow).

## Radiation & energy spread
- Inverse-square law for point-source radiative intensity in 3D — the classical result SUG's logarithmic ansatz must be reconciled with (see open question O2).

## Energy–mass & high-energy phenomena
- Einstein, A. (1905). On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies; and the later energy–mass equivalence *E = mc²* — energy as a fundamental book-keeping quantity.
- Literature on blast waves and high-energy detonation scaling (e.g. the Taylor–von Neumann–Sedov self-similar blast solution) as a classical counterpoint to SUG's vacuum destruction-radius claims.

## Mathematics used
- Logarithmic integral :math:`\operatorname{li}(x)` and its Euler-series representation (special functions; Abramowitz & Stegun, *Handbook of Mathematical Functions*).
- Elementary dimensional analysis and the method of reference scales / non-dimensionalisation (Buckingham π theorem family).
